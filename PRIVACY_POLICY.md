# Privacy Policy

One Second Diary takes your privacy seriously. The app collects nothing about you, and everything you make with it
stays on your phone.


## Data Collection

No data is collected.

- There is no account, no server, no analytics, no tracking and no crash reporting service.
- Your videos, photos, movies, profiles, subtitles, settings and logs are stored only on your phone. On Android they
  are ordinary files in `DCIM/OneSecondDiary`; on iPhone they are in the app's folder, which you can open in the Files
  app. Your phone's own backup may include them, depending on your backup settings.
- From version 2.0.0, the Android app no longer has the internet permission, so it cannot connect to the internet by
  itself. The iPhone app contains no code that sends your data anywhere.


## Location (optional)

Geotagging is off unless you turn it on.

- When it is on, the app reads your position while you save a clip and asks your phone's location service to turn it
  into a place name. That service belongs to your phone (Google or your phone's maker on Android, Apple on iPhone), and
  it may look the place name up online under its own privacy policy. The app itself sends nothing.
- The place name is drawn on the video, and the position is written into the video file.
- You can type a place yourself instead. Then no position is read or looked up, and none is written into the file.


## Permissions

The app asks for these permissions, each for one purpose:

- Camera and microphone: to record your clips.
- Photos and videos (storage): to save your diary, add videos or photos from your gallery, and make movies.
- Location: only for geotagging, described above, and only when you turn it on.
- Notifications: only for the daily reminder, and only when you turn it on.


## Sharing and Contact

Nothing leaves your phone unless you choose to send it.

- When you share a clip or a movie, your phone's share menu sends the file to the app you pick. The file contains what
  you see in it (the date, subtitles, the place if you added one) and, if geotagging was on, the position.
- "Report error" and "Contact" open your own email app with a message to the developer that you can read, change or
  discard. A report attaches the app's logs from the last 7 days: technical events and errors, which can include the
  dates of your clips and the names of your profiles. The logs are sent only if you send that email.
- Links in Settings (source code, support, the backup tutorial) open in your browser. Those websites have their own
  privacy policies.


## Advertisement and Other Third Parties

There are none.
